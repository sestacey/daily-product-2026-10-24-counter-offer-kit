Counter Offer Kit

Negotiate your job offer without risking it. Most people who negotiate get a better offer — and most people never ask, because they're afraid of losing the job. This kit turns a scary conversation into a plan: diagnose your position, run the real total-compensation math, generate your counter-offer, and validate it before you send anything.

Built for job seekers with a written offer in hand. Not legal advice and not a guarantee of a higher offer — a preparation tool. Works for salaried roles; adapt the numbers for hourly roles by annualizing.

Part 1 — Diagnostic: How strong is your position?

Answer honestly. Your score tells you how hard to push — and where the risk actually is.

Part 2 — Generator A: Total compensation calculator

Base salary is only one lever. Compare the offer against your target on total value — then pick the cheapest lever for them to move.

Part 2 — Generator B: Your counter-offer, written for you

Fill in the blanks, then copy the email. Rule of thumb baked in: counter 5–15% above the offer, never as an ultimatum, always as a question.

Part 3 — Validator: Ready-to-send checklist

Do not send until every box is honestly checked. If any box fails, fix it first — that's the whole point.

The 5-minute script (if they call instead)

"Thank you again for the offer — I'm really excited about the team and the role. I've been reviewing the full package, and based on [market data / competing timeline / scope of the role], I was expecting something closer to $[counter]. Is there flexibility to move in that direction? If base is fixed, I'd love to talk about [fallback ask]."

Then stop talking. Silence is not rudeness — it's letting them answer. Never apologize for asking, and never say "I'll walk" unless you mean it.
